Privacy Policy

RawBridge is designed to convert camera RAW files to DNG locally on your device.

Summary

RawBridge does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, or telemetry. RawBridge does not operate a server that receives your photos, RAW files, converted DNGs, settings, or diagnostics.

Files and conversion

Files you select are read only for the purpose of reviewing and converting them. RAW decoding and DNG creation take place on your iPhone or iPad. RawBridge does not upload the selected files or their contents to a RawBridge service.

Converted files are written only to the destination you choose, such as your Photos library or a folder selected through the system file picker. Access to those destinations is managed by iOS and iPadOS.

Photo metadata

Camera files can contain metadata such as camera model, lens, capture settings, timestamps, serial numbers, copyright details, and existing location coordinates. RawBridge may preserve selected metadata in a converted DNG according to your metadata settings.

RawBridge does not request location permission, determine your current location, or add newly measured location data. Turning off location preservation prevents existing location metadata from being copied to the output where supported.

Settings and diagnostics

App preferences are stored locally on your device. If you enable diagnostics, RawBridge can store a limited local log containing information such as filenames, camera models, conversion settings, timings, storage status, and error details. Diagnostic logs do not contain the RAW image payload.

Diagnostics remain on your device until you clear them or remove the app. They leave the device only when you deliberately use the Share Diagnostics control and choose a destination or recipient.

Data collection and sharing

RawBridge does not collect, sell, rent, or share personal information. It does not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites.

Apple services you choose to use, including iCloud, Photos, Files, TestFlight, and App Store services, are governed by Apple’s own terms and privacy policies.

Website hosting

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process basic connection information, such as an IP address, for security and service operation under the GitHub Privacy Statement. This website does not use custom analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when RawBridge’s features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page identifies the latest published version.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to rawbridgeapp@gmail.com.
