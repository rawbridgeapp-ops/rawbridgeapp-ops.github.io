RawBridge Support

Help with importing camera RAW files and converting them to DNG.

Contact

Email rawbridgeapp@gmail.com with a short description of the problem, your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, camera model, and RAW storage mode.

Please do not attach private photos unless they are necessary to reproduce the issue and you are comfortable sharing them.

Compatibility

RawBridge requires iOS or iPadOS 18 or later. Supported input families currently include RAF, ARW, NEF, NRW, CR2, CR3, ORF, RW2, SRW, and PEF.

Only camera models listed in the app’s Supported Cameras page are validated. RawBridge supports uncompressed and lossless RAW storage modes; lossy or otherwise unsupported storage modes may not convert.

Before contacting support

  1. Check that the exact camera model appears under Settings → Supported Cameras.
  2. Confirm that the source is an uncompressed or lossless RAW rather than a lossy RAW mode.
  3. Make sure the device and selected output destination have enough free storage.
  4. Try converting one file with the default settings.
  5. If the problem continues, enable Diagnostics, repeat the conversion, and use Share Diagnostics in the app.

Background conversion

Short conversions may finish while another app is in use. iOS controls background execution time and may pause longer work. Reopen RawBridge to continue when necessary. Keep RawBridge open for important or unusually large batches.

Privacy

RawBridge processes files locally and does not upload them to a RawBridge service. Read the full Privacy Policy.
